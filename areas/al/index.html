Local conditions across Alabama

Summers in Alabama are hot and humid, and that combination changes how a garage door behaves. Steel expands, wooden panels swell, lubricant thins out and drips away, and the opener works in temperatures that push its motor and electronics harder than in cooler parts of the country.

In Alabama the most common service calls in July and August are doors that close unevenly, openers that stop halfway, and safety sensors that trip because of heat, insects or low voltage.

What we see most in Alabama

  • Expansion and binding. Heat expands metal panels and tracks, which can make the door drag at the jambs.
  • Opener overheating. Motors and control boards run hotter in an unventilated garage and can shut down mid-cycle.
  • Safety sensor faults. Sunlight, dirt, spider webs and heat-related voltage dips all interrupt the safety beam.
  • Wood swelling and rot. Humidity swells wooden sections; untreated or poorly sealed wood develops rot around the bottom rail.
  • Dry or melted lubrication. Thin or dried-out grease on rollers, hinges and springs accelerates wear.
