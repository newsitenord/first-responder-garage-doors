Local conditions across Oregon

Oregon gets long wet seasons rather than hard freezes. Rain drives into every gap, keeps steel damp and encourages moss and algae on the door and apron. Wooden doors are especially vulnerable because they never get a chance to dry out fully.

In Oregon, the recurring repairs are rotten bottom rails, failed seals, rusty tracks and doors that swell and stick in the wettest months.

What we see most in Oregon

  • Rot in wooden doors. Persistent moisture attacks the bottom rail and joints first.
  • Rust on tracks and cables. Constant damp shortens the life of bare steel parts.
  • Failed bottom seals. Standing water at the apron destroys seals and lets rain into the garage.
  • Moss and algae. Growth on the door and apron traps moisture against the surface.
  • Swelling and binding. Wooden sections swell in wet months and drag in the opening.
