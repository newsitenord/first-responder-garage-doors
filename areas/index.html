Frequently asked questions

How do I check if you cover my address?

Find your state and city in the directory, then call the dispatch line with your full address. Coverage is confirmed against the partner network before an appointment is booked.

Why are some cities listed but not others?

The network covers the cities on this page. Neighbouring towns are often covered by the same technicians — call and we will check your ZIP code against the nearest partner.

Do prices change by city?

The structure is the same everywhere, but labour rates and call-out fees vary by market. Your city page lists typical ranges and the technician confirms the exact price on site.
