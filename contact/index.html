Frequently asked questions

What should I have ready before calling?

Your address or ZIP code, the door's make if you know it, and a description of what happens when you press the button. If the door is stuck open, say so first — it changes how the call is prioritised.

How soon will someone call me back?

Requests submitted through the form are routed to the dispatch desk during business hours and to the on-call line after hours. Urgent requests should always go through the phone line.

Can I get an estimate without booking?

Installation estimates are free. Repair pricing is confirmed on site after diagnosis; some markets charge a small diagnostic fee that is disclosed before dispatch.
