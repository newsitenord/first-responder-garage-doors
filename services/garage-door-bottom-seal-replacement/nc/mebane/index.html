What we handle in Mebane

The bottom seal is a rubber or vinyl gasket that runs along the bottom edge of the lowest panel, compressing against the floor when the door closes. There are several profiles: C-channel slide-in, bulb, U-shape, T-end and blade seals. The right profile depends on the door's retainer.

  • Old seal removal and retainer cleaning
  • Correct profile matched to your door
  • New seal cut to width and fitted
  • Bottom panel and apron inspection
  • Adjustment of the door's down limit
  • Optional threshold or apron seal advice

When to call for bottom seal in Mebane

  • Water pooling at the garage floor after rain

  • Mice or insects getting in under the door
  • The bottom panel is rusting along its lower edge

Local conditions that affect doors in Mebane

Because Alamance County also sees tropical storms and heavy rain events, we treat wind-rated hardware and a properly sealed perimeter as part of the job, not an upgrade.

Rust and corrosion

High humidity keeps bare steel damp; hinges, rollers and bottom brackets corrode quickly without regular lubrication.

Swollen or warped wood doors

Moisture absorption changes the shape of wooden panels and can jam them in the opening.

Seal and weatherstrip failure

UV exposure and heat make vinyl seals brittle, and the first storm pushes water straight in.

Storm wind pressure

Loose tracks, hinges and anchors are the first thing to fail in a high-wind event.

Timing note: Inspect and lubricate before hurricane season, and re-check the perimeter seals afterwards; storm-driven rain finds every gap.

Door choice: Choose a wind-rated door with factory-applied finish and stainless or galvanized hardware, and skip unfinished wood in exposed openings.

Before the technician arrives: Keep the area around the tracks clear and avoid storing items against the door panels. Bent tracks from stored ladders and bikes are one of the most common avoidable repairs.

Bottom Seal in Mebane: common questions

How often should a bottom seal be replaced?

Every 3–5 years in most climates, sooner if the door faces direct sun or the floor drains poorly.

Can I just buy a universal seal?

Universal seals fit some retainers but leave gaps on others. Matching the profile to your door is what makes the seal watertight.

Will a new seal stop water coming in?

It stops water that comes in under the door. If water runs toward the garage from the driveway, an apron or threshold seal is the next step.
