What we handle in Clarkston

A garage door tune-up is a preventive service, not a repair. It includes lubrication of springs, hinges, rollers and bearings; balance testing; track alignment; tightening of hardware; adjustment of opener travel and force limits; and testing of the safety reverse system. Anything found worn is quoted separately before it is replaced.

  • Lubrication of springs, hinges, rollers, bearings and tracks
  • Balance test with the opener disconnected
  • Track alignment and hardware tightening
  • Roller and hinge wear inspection
  • Safety reverse and photo-eye test
  • Opener travel and force adjustment

When to call for tune-up in Clarkston

Local conditions that affect doors in Clarkston

In DeKalb County the most common service calls in July and August are doors that close unevenly, openers that stop halfway, and safety sensors that trip because of heat, insects or low voltage.

Expansion and binding

Heat expands metal panels and tracks, which can make the door drag at the jambs.

Opener overheating

Motors and control boards run hotter in an unventilated garage and can shut down mid-cycle.

Safety sensor faults

Sunlight, dirt, spider webs and heat-related voltage dips all interrupt the safety beam.

Wood swelling and rot

Humidity swells wooden sections; untreated or poorly sealed wood develops rot around the bottom rail.

Timing note: Late spring is the best time for a tune-up here: adjust travel limits, clean and align the safety sensors and re-lubricate before the heat sets in.

Door choice: Insulated steel or aluminum with a factory finish copes with heat and humidity better than wood and keeps the garage noticeably cooler.

Before the technician arrives: Note the door's make and the colour of the springs before the technician arrives. It speeds up parts selection and often shortens the visit by 20–30 minutes.

Tune-Up in Clarkston: common questions

How often should a garage door be tuned up?

Once a year for most homes, and twice a year if the door gets heavy daily use or the climate swings hard between seasons.

Is a tune-up worth it if the door works fine?

Yes. The point is to catch worn rollers, loose hardware and weak springs before they fail and take a cable or opener with them.
