What we handle in College Park

A garage door tune-up is a preventive service, not a repair. It includes lubrication of springs, hinges, rollers and bearings; balance testing; track alignment; tightening of hardware; adjustment of opener travel and force limits; and testing of the safety reverse system. Anything found worn is quoted separately before it is replaced.

  • Lubrication of springs, hinges, rollers, bearings and tracks
  • Balance test with the opener disconnected
  • Track alignment and hardware tightening
  • Roller and hinge wear inspection
  • Safety reverse and photo-eye test
  • Opener travel and force adjustment

When to call for tune-up in College Park

  • The door has not been serviced in more than a year
  • It sounds louder or rougher than it used to
  • The door shudders or hesitates when it starts to move
  • The opener's light blinks or the door reverses occasionally
  • You are about to sell or buy a house with a garage
  • Winter or storm season is coming

Local conditions that affect doors in College Park

Because Prince George's County also sees tropical storms and heavy rain events, we treat wind-rated hardware and a properly sealed perimeter as part of the job, not an upgrade.

Rust and corrosion

High humidity keeps bare steel damp; hinges, rollers and bottom brackets corrode quickly without regular lubrication.

Swollen or warped wood doors

Moisture absorption changes the shape of wooden panels and can jam them in the opening.

Seal and weatherstrip failure

UV exposure and heat make vinyl seals brittle, and the first storm pushes water straight in.

Storm wind pressure

Loose tracks, hinges and anchors are the first thing to fail in a high-wind event.

Timing note: Inspect and lubricate before hurricane season, and re-check the perimeter seals afterwards; storm-driven rain finds every gap.

Door choice: Choose a wind-rated door with factory-applied finish and stainless or galvanized hardware, and skip unfinished wood in exposed openings.

Before the technician arrives: Test the safety reverse monthly: place a roll of paper towels under the door and close it. If the door does not reverse on contact, the opener needs adjustment before someone gets hurt.

Tune-Up in College Park: common questions

How often should a garage door be tuned up?

Once a year for most homes, and twice a year if the door gets heavy daily use or the climate swings hard between seasons.

Is a tune-up worth it if the door works fine?

Yes. The point is to catch worn rollers, loose hardware and weak springs before they fail and take a cable or opener with them.

What is actually included?

Lubrication, balance testing, track alignment, hardware tightening, safety-reverse testing and opener limit and force adjustment. Parts are quoted separately.
