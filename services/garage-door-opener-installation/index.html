A new opener is only as good as its installation. The rail has to be level, the header bracket has to be anchored into structure rather than trim, the travel limits have to match the door's actual open and closed positions, and the force settings have to be calibrated to a door that is properly balanced. Get any of those wrong and the opener will be noisy, unreliable or unsafe.

We install belt, chain and screw-drive openers across your area and your county, including Wi-Fi models, battery-backup units and openers paired with a new door. Every install finishes with a full safety-reverse test at the floor.

What opener install involves

Opener installation covers mounting the header bracket and rail, hanging the motor from the ceiling structure, attaching the trolley to the door, wiring the safety sensors, connecting the wall console and programming remotes and travel limits. It also includes confirming the door is balanced — an opener should never be used to fix a heavy door.

What is included

  • Door balance check before mounting
  • Opener supply, rail assembly and ceiling mounting
  • Safety sensor installation and alignment
  • Wall console and remote programming
  • Travel limit and force calibration
  • Full safety-reverse and obstacle test

Signs you need this service

  • The existing opener is loud, slow or struggles with the door
  • You have no opener and are tired of opening the door by hand
  • You want Wi-Fi control, scheduling or voice assistant support
  • You need a battery backup for power cuts
  • A new door was installed and the old opener is underpowered
  • The old unit has no working safety reverse

How the visit works

  1. 1

    Check the door

    A door that does not balance properly is adjusted before any opener is fitted, or the new unit will fail early.

  2. 2

    Choose the drive type

    Belt for quiet rooms above the garage, chain for budget and durability, screw-drive for low maintenance, plus Wi-Fi and battery-backup options.

  3. 3

    Mount and wire

    The rail, motor, sensors and console are installed and wired neatly, with the header bracket anchored into solid framing.

  4. 4

    Calibrate and test

    Limits and force are set, remotes paired and the reverse test completed on the floor.

Opener Install questions

Which opener is best for a bedroom above the garage?

A belt-drive opener is the quietest option and is the usual choice where a room sits above or beside the garage. Chain-drive units cost less but transmit more noise.

How long does installation take?

A straight replacement on an existing rail is typically 1–2 hours. A new installation with sensors, console wiring and a new rail takes 2–4 hours.

Can you install an opener I bought online?

Yes, in most cases, but labour-only installation usually carries a limited workmanship warranty and the unit warranty stays with the retailer.

Do I need a battery backup?

If you rely on the garage as your main entry, or you live where outages are common, a battery-backup opener is worth it. It also lets you open the door during a power cut.

Will a new opener work with my old remotes?

Usually not, because manufacturers change frequency and security protocols. New remotes are paired during installation and extra ones can be supplied.
