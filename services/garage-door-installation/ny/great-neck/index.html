What we handle in Great Neck

Garage door installation means removing the old door and hardware, preparing the opening, installing new tracks, springs, drums, cables and panels, then connecting and calibrating the opener. The door, springs and opener have to be matched to each other — a spring rated for the wrong door weight is the most common reason a new door feels heavy or fails early.

  • On-site measurement of opening, headroom and jamb conditions
  • Door and spring selection matched to door weight and cycle life
  • Removal and disposal of the old door and hardware
  • New tracks, hinges, rollers, cables, drums and brackets
  • Opener mounting and safety-reverse calibration
  • Final balance test, weather seal fitting and site clean-up

Local conditions that affect doors in Great Neck

When water gets into a track or behind a weather seal and refreezes overnight, the door can freeze to the slab. Forcing an opener through a frozen door is one of the most common causes of stripped gears and snapped lift cables we see in Nassau County.

Cables and springs failing in cold snaps

Steel contracts in low temperatures and torsion springs that are near the end of their cycle life often break on the first hard freeze of the season.

Doors frozen to the floor

Meltwater under the bottom seal refreezes overnight, bonding the door to the concrete. The opener pulls against it and the trolley, gear or cable gives first.

Salt corrosion on hinges and rollers

Rusty hinges and dry nylon rollers create the grinding and shuddering that homeowners notice most in winter.

Track misalignment from frost heave

Ground movement and ice at the door opening can push a track out of plumb, which then drags on the panels.

Timing note: Book a pre-winter tune-up in early autumn: lubrication, balance check, spring cycle inspection and a bottom-seal test before the first freeze.

Door choice: Insulated steel or composite doors hold up best here; whatever the material, specify sealed bearings and corrosion-resistant hinges for a salt-exposed garage.

Before the technician arrives: Before you call, check whether the door is locked, whether the opener is unplugged, and whether anything is blocking the safety beam. About one in ten "dead door" calls is a tripped breaker or a sensor knocked out of line.

Installation in Great Neck: common questions

How long does a garage door installation take?

A standard single or double door is usually a one-day job for two technicians. Custom wood doors, doors with new framing or opener replacements can extend into a second day.

Do I need a permit in Great Neck?

Permit rules vary by municipality and by whether the opening is being altered structurally. We tell you what applies to your address before scheduling, and the local installer handles documentation where required.

What is the difference between insulated and non-insulated doors?

Insulated doors have a foam core bonded to the panel, which improves temperature control, reduces noise and stiffens the door. For attached garages, insulation is usually worth the extra cost.
