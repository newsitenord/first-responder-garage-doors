Local conditions that affect doors in Happy Valley

In Clackamas County, the recurring repairs are rotten bottom rails, failed seals, rusty tracks and doors that swell and stick in the wettest months.

Rot in wooden doors

Persistent moisture attacks the bottom rail and joints first.

Rust on tracks and cables

Constant damp shortens the life of bare steel parts.

Failed bottom seals

Standing water at the apron destroys seals and lets rain into the garage.

Moss and algae

Growth on the door and apron traps moisture against the surface.

Timing note: Inspect the bottom rail and seals before the rainy season, and keep the apron clear so water drains away from the door.

Door choice: Fiberglass, aluminum or steel with a factory finish avoids the rot problem entirely in a wet climate.

Before the technician arrives: Test the safety reverse monthly: place a roll of paper towels under the door and close it. If the door does not reverse on contact, the opener needs adjustment before someone gets hurt.

Which door material is best for a house in Happy Valley?

For most homes, insulated steel is the best balance of cost, durability and efficiency. Wood and full-view glass look excellent but need more maintenance in this climate.

It depends on the municipality and whether the opening changes. We check the local requirement for your address before the install date.

How long does installation take?
