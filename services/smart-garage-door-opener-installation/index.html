A smart opener lets you open the garage for a delivery, check whether the door was left open and give a family member temporary access — all from a phone. The installation is only half the job: the opener has to be paired to the right Wi-Fi band, the app has to be configured properly and the safety sensors still have to work exactly as before.

We install and configure Wi-Fi openers in your area and your county, including MyQ-compatible units, battery-backup models and openers that work with Alexa, Google Home and Apple Home through the manufacturer's bridge.

What smart opener involves

A smart opener is a garage door operator with built-in Wi-Fi or a module that connects it to the manufacturer's cloud service. It adds app control, open/closed status, activity alerts and integration with voice assistants. The mechanical parts — motor, rail, springs, sensors — still matter as much as the connectivity.

  • 2.4 GHz network configuration and app pairing

Smart Opener questions

Will a smart opener work with my Wi-Fi?

Most require a 2.4 GHz network. If your router publishes a combined network, the technician configures the opener correctly or advises on a separate 2.4 GHz SSID.

Can I add smart control to my existing opener?

Often yes, with a manufacturer module or a compatible controller, provided the opener has the right terminals and works reliably.

Is a smart opener a security risk?

Modern units use encrypted rolling codes and cloud accounts. Use a strong password and two-factor authentication on the app, and remove guest access when it is no longer needed.

Does it work with Alexa or Google?

Many models do, directly or through the manufacturer's integration. The technician confirms compatibility before the unit is ordered.

What happens in a power cut?

Battery-backup models keep working for a limited number of cycles; standard units release with the manual disconnect cord.
